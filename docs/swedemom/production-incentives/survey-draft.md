# Production bonus survey (draft for manager review)

Anonymous Google Form, English only, about 5 to 7 minutes, open Oct 12-16. Built by `create-survey-form.gs` (same questions as below). Only Jamie sees raw answers; results are shared as totals.

**Form intro:** We're thinking about adding a production bonus on top of your hourly wage, and we want your honest take before anything is decided. This survey is anonymous: it does not collect your name or email, and only overall results are shared. It takes about 5 to 7 minutes. Please read the proposal first (your manager has a copy).

## Section 1: About your work (optional)

1. **Which best describes most of your work?** (multiple choice, optional)
   Intake, description or imaging / Sorting / Final check, put-away or fulfillment / A mix of several jobs / Prefer not to say

## Section 2: Overall

2. **Overall, how do you feel about a production bonus like the one in the proposal?** (scale 1-5: Very negative to Very positive)
3. **How clear was the proposal?** (scale 1-5: Very confusing to Very clear)
4. **What do you like most about the idea?** (paragraph, optional)
5. **What worries you, if anything?** (checkboxes, optional, with Other)
   - People might rush and quality could drop
   - The bar might not be fair
   - Some items or bins are harder than others
   - Tracking time could feel like being watched
   - It could cause tension between coworkers
   - Team goals depend on things I can't control
   - It's too complicated
   - The rules might change later
   - Nothing worries me

## Section 3: How it should work

6. **If we had a set amount to spend on bonuses, how should it be split?** (multiple choice)
   Mostly individual bonuses / About half individual, half team / Mostly team bonuses / Not sure
7. **Above the bar, you keep half of the time you save and Swedemom keeps half. How does that feel?** (multiple choice)
   Fair / Too little for us / Not sure
8. **How motivating would a weekly team goal for your stage be?** (scale 1-5: Not at all to Very)
9. **If you work in more than one stage, your team bonus would follow your mix of work (for example 60% imaging, 40% intake). Does that seem fair?** (multiple choice)
    Yes / No / Not sure / Doesn't apply to me
10. **How often would you like to see your progress?** (multiple choice)
    Live in the Hub during the day / Once a day / Once a week / Only on payday
11. **Where would you rather clock in and out?** (multiple choice)
    In the Hub, with my production work / In Gusto, like now / No preference
12. **How would you feel about tapping a timer in the Hub for other work (truck runs, wrapping glass, meetings)?** (multiple choice)
    Easy, no problem / A little annoying / Very annoying / Not sure
13. **Which other roles should we work on including?** (checkboxes, with Other)
    Final Check / Verification

## Section 4: In your words

14. **What would make a bonus like this feel fair to you?** (paragraph, optional)
15. **What slows you down most at work that we could fix?** (paragraph, optional)
16. **Anything else you want us to know?** (paragraph, optional)

**Confirmation message:** Thank you! Your answers are anonymous. We'll share what we heard, and the updated plan, before anything changes.

## Notes for managers

- Q11 helps decide Hub vs Gusto for clocking. Q15 doubles as a list of tool fixes to prioritize.
- Only Q2, Q3 and Q6 are required, so nobody is forced into an answer they're unsure of.
- Anonymity at ~20 people: Q1 is the only demographic, it is optional, and results for any group under 5 people are not broken out.
