# Production bonus survey (v3 draft for manager review, 2026-10-14)

Anonymous Google Form, English only, about 8 to 10 minutes, open for one week from the day it's emailed. Built by `create-survey-form.gs` (same questions as below). Only Jamie sees raw answers; results are shared as totals.

**What changed in v3 (Jamie, 2026-10-14):**
- **Branching.** Everyone picks the kinds of work they do and their main job, then sees one page about their main job.
- **Criticism always gets a "why".** Negative or "rarely" answers lead to a short required follow-up, framed as "what would make it better".
- **Necessary parts are explained, not voted on.** Time tracking gets a page intro saying why it's needed, then asks how to make it painless.
- **New intake questions:** which intake methods people use (camera with a phone or webcam, barcode, exact match, keyword, typing) and why.
- **New station questions** (Cami and Devri, Slack 2026-10-14): what's missing at your station.
- **New question:** "How should we start?" covers Devri's team-goals-first idea.

**Form intro:** We're thinking about adding a production bonus on top of your hourly wage, and we want your honest take before anything is decided, including criticism. This survey is anonymous: it does not collect your name or email, and only overall results are shared. It takes about 8 to 10 minutes. Please read the proposal first (it's attached to the email with this survey).

## 1. Your work (everyone)

1. **Which kinds of work do you do? Select all that apply.** (checkboxes, optional): Sorting / Intake + description / Imaging / Put-away / Fulfillment (pulling, packing, shipping) / Other work (store, truck, office, inventory checks) / Prefer not to say
2. **Which one is your main job?** (required, same choices). Routes to:
   - 2a: Sorting
   - 2b: Intake
   - 2c: Imaging
   - 2d: Put-away or Fulfillment
   - 2e: Other
   - Prefer not to say skips to section 3.

## 2a. Sorting
3. **What slows sorting down the most?** (checkboxes, Other): Deciding what is worth listing / The Sort tool is slow or awkward / Not enough space or carts / Too many low-value items to get through / Clothing and textiles are hard to judge / Nothing much
4. **How often do you use the Sort tool when you sort?** (required): On almost every item / Sometimes / Rarely / Never
   - Sometimes, Rarely or Never leads to a follow-up:
     - **What keeps you from using the Sort tool more?** (checkboxes, required, Other): It's slower than eyeballing the box / Its price estimates feel wrong / I don't have a phone or device handy / I haven't been shown how / I'm not sure it matters
     - **In your own words, why? What would get you to use it on most items?** (paragraph, required)

## 3. The plan (everyone)
17. **How clear was the proposal?** (scale 1-5, required: Very confusing to Very clear)
18. **Which parts of the idea appeal to you most?** (checkboxes, Other): Earning more when I clear the bar / The weekly team goal / My wage never goes down / Being measured against the bar, not coworkers / Seeing my progress live in the Hub / Fast feedback on my work, with photos when something goes wrong / Better tools and equipment before anything is paid / A practice run before real money
19. **Overall, how do you feel about a production bonus like the one in the proposal?** (required): Very positive / Somewhat positive / Neutral / Somewhat negative / Very negative
   - Somewhat or Very negative leads to a follow-up: **What would need to change for this to feel good to you?** (paragraph, required)
20. **If we had a set amount to spend on bonuses, how should it be split?** (required): Mostly individual bonuses / About half individual, half team / Mostly team bonuses / Not sure
21. **How should we start?** Team goals and individual bonuses together / Team goals first, individual bonuses later / Not sure
22. **How motivating would a weekly team goal for your stage be?** (scale 1-5: Not at all to Very)
23. **If you work in more than one stage, your team bonus would follow your mix of work (for example 60% imaging, 40% intake). Does that seem fair?** Yes / No / Not sure / Doesn't apply to me
24. **How often would you like to see your progress?** Live in the Hub during the day / Once a day / Once a week / Only on payday
25. **Above the bar, you keep half of the time you save and Swedemom keeps half. How does that feel?** (required): Fair / Too little for us / Not sure
   - Too little leads to a follow-up: **What share would feel fair to you, and why?** (paragraph, required)

**Tracking time (page intro):** To pay a bonus fairly, we need accurate production hours. That means clocking in and out, and a quick way to mark time spent on other work (truck runs, wrapping glass, meetings) so it never counts against your pace. Breaks are handled automatically. We want this to be as painless as possible.

26. **Where would clocking in and out be easiest for you?** In the Hub, with my production work / In Gusto, like now / No preference
27. **How would a one-tap timer in the Hub for other work feel?** (required): Easy, no problem / A little annoying / Very annoying / Not sure
   - A little or Very annoying leads to a follow-up: **What would make it less annoying, or how else could we track that time fairly?** (paragraph, required). The page intro says we do need some way to track it.

**Confirmation message:** Thank you! Your answers are anonymous. We'll share what we heard, and the updated plan, before anything changes.

## Notes for managers
- **Length:** a typical person sees about 17 to 20 questions.
- **Necessary evils:** they are explained, then the survey asks how to make them painless. They are never put to a yes/no vote.
- **Anonymity:** Q2 routes people, so it is required, but it includes "Prefer not to say". Results for any group under 5 people are not broken out.
