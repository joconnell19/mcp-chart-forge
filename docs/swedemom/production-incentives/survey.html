The questions we'd ask.

The survey we propose sending to everyone with the production bonus proposal. It branches: each person answers questions about their own main job, and a few short follow-ups appear only when someone answers "rarely" or negatively, so we learn why. Please read it as a staff member would and tell us what to change, add or cut.

Notes for managers

  • Nobody answers all 30. A typical person sees about 17 to 20: the two opening questions, one stage page, the shared plan questions, and a follow-up only when they answer "rarely" or negatively.
  • Criticism always gets a "why". Negative feelings about the plan, a low share, an annoying timer, or rarely using the Sort tool or camera intake each lead to a short required follow-up, framed as "what would make it better".
  • Necessary parts are explained, not put to a vote. Tracking hours is required for a fair bonus, so the survey explains why and asks how to make it painless, rather than whether to do it.
  • The intake page asks which methods people use and why, including keyword search versus camera intake, and what's missing at their station. That feeds the station equipment plan (dedicated phones, faster computers).
  • The main job question routes people, so it is required, with "Prefer not to say". Results for any group smaller than 5 people are never shown separately, and only Jamie sees the raw answers. Everyone gets the overall results.
