Notes for managers

  • Question 10 helps us decide between clocking in the Hub and in Gusto. Question 12 asks how to reward inventory checks and stale-inventory cleanup. Question 14 doubles as a list of tool fixes to prioritize.
  • Only questions 2, 3 and 5 are required, so nobody is pushed into an answer they're unsure of.
  • With about 20 people, question 1 is the only one about who you are, and it's optional. Results for any group smaller than 5 people are never shown separately.
  • Only Jamie sees the raw answers. Everyone gets the overall results.
